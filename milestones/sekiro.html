  1. 一

    井底

    New Game · Ashina Reservoir11-21

    成就11-21 · 从标题菜单到爬出井底，全程无人操作：114 秒，蹬墙 9 轮。

    问题区域名横幅在坑里也会弹出，脚本以为“已经出坑”，发出去的视频只好撤回。一口气改好几处、每次只测两三回，约 20 次只成 1 次。

    解法出坑的判据换成“画面里看得到天空”。固定基线，一次只改一处，录满 6 次再下结论：3/6 → 5/6。

  2. 二

    蓄水池潜行

    Reservoir · Stealth11-05 — 11-08

    成就11-06 · 潜过守卫，钻进地板下的通道。

    问题成批重试，成功率在 0/6 和 4/6 之间乱跳，看不出原因；守卫一发现，这一趟就完了。

    解法不用神经网络，用参考帧的梯度特征跟路。识别敌人头顶的警戒标记：白 → 黄 → 红，比挨打早 4–7 秒，足够躲进屋底。一次失败逐帧分析，不再成批重试。

  3. 三

    悬崖与断桥

    Cliff Traverse · Kuro's Room11-09

    成就11-09 夜 · 横移过悬崖、跳过断桥，进屋拿到楔丸。

    问题凭想象猜地形，白费约 15 次；断桥试了 5 种跳法，全掉下去。

    解法项目负责人一句“就是 D 往前推”——挂在崖边横着挪。把参考视频切成 0.1 秒一帧，看清起跳后 0.3 秒要按抓边。

  4. 四

    山内重则

    忍殺

    Shigenori Yamauchi11-09 — 11-10

    成就11-10 · 首次击杀山内重则，第一个有名字的武将。新档重走时第一次就赢。

    问题一直按住防御只是格挡，两个杂兵 20–30 秒就把狼打死。一下午约 25 次，0 胜。

    解法查资料才知道弹反要“重新按下”防御键。不理杂兵，直冲武将，打的时候保持移动。

  5. 五

    寺院与忍义手

    Genichiro · Dilapidated Temple11-11

    成就11-11 · 走完对源一郎的剧情败战，到达荒废寺院，装上忍义手。

    问题按键回放是开环的，三次过一次。战斗后镜头朝下，盲走偏了 622 像素，掉下去两回。步骤打印的“OK”没有任何证据。

    解法战斗前先拍一张“前进方向”的画面，打完照着转回去。每一步的“OK”都要有屏幕上的正面证据。

  6. 六

    河原田直盛

    忍殺

    General Naomori Kawarada11-11 — 11-12

    成就11-12 17:16 · 击杀河原田直盛。

    问题落到门楼屋顶，4 次有 3 次被看见；把“危”字当成了忍杀红点；正面交手，五场输四场。

    解法学会看钩点：带斜杠的不能用，实心的才行。先蹲 20 秒再跳下落杀；正面按住防御，每 0.3 秒一刀。

    忍殺

  10. 十

    大蛇谷

    Great Serpent11-23 — 11-24

    成就11-23 · 第一次刺中蛇眼。11-24 05:14 · 过大蛇谷，到城门要塞鬼佛。

    解法发现蛇身约每 20 秒扫过一次：在它“开始扫过”的瞬间出发。刺眼之后的路线做成一口气的固定动作。

    解法承认逃课无效，回来正面打。正面要打赢，得先有爆竹。

一路学到的六件事

引号里是项目负责人的原话

  • 先分析，再重试

    “不要再随机重试了，要多分析。”

    一帧一帧找原因，一次只改一处。

  • 用便宜的办法

    “太贵了，用传统的方法。”

    全程是经典图像处理和脚本，没有一个神经网络。

  • 写成闭环

    “开环的总是会失败的，基本过不去的。”

    地标 → 动作 → 检查，用看得见的事件代替定时器。

  • 自己留锚点

    “战斗之前一定要标好方向，记下一个帧来。”

    自己存的画面，比别人的视频好认得多。

  • 动作要在屏幕上看到

    读葫芦数，读纸人数。

    十分钟的按键测试，解决了三天统计没解决的问题。

  • 多进攻

    “多进攻，不要总想防守，进攻才能干掉敌人。”

    对手血越少，架势涨得越快。

  1. 一

    The Well

    井底Nov 21

    ReachedNov 21 · From the title menu to the top of the well with no human input: 114 s, nine rounds of wall kicks.

    ProblemThe area-name banner also shows inside the pit, so the script reported “out” while still in it and a posted video had to be withdrawn. Changing several things at once and testing two or three times each: about 1 success in 20.

    Fix“Out of the pit” now means open sky in the frame. Fix a baseline, change one thing, record six runs before judging: 3/6 → 5/6.

  2. 二

    Reservoir Stealth

    蓄水池潜行Nov 5 — Nov 8

    ReachedNov 6 · Past the guards and into the crawl space under the floor.

    ProblemRetrying in batches gave anything from 0/6 to 4/6 with no visible cause; once a guard noticed the Wolf, the run was over.

    FixNo neural network: follow the route by gradient features of reference frames. Read the alert marker over an enemy's head, white → yellow → red; it shows 4–7 s before the attack, time enough to hide under the house. Analyse one failure frame by frame instead of retrying in batches.

  3. 三

    Cliff and Broken Bridge

    悬崖与断桥Nov 9

    ReachedNight of Nov 9 · Along the cliff, across the broken bridge, into Kuro's room: the sword Kusabimaru.

    ProblemGuessing the geometry wasted about 15 attempts; five ways of jumping the broken bridge all fell.

    FixOne line from the project lead, “just push D”: hang on the ledge and shimmy. Cutting the reference video into 0.1-s frames showed that the grab key goes 0.3 s after the jump.

  4. 四

    Shigenori Yamauchi

    忍殺

    山内重则Nov 9 — Nov 10

    ReachedNov 10 · First kill of Shigenori Yamauchi, the first named general. On the later new-game run he fell at the first try.

    ProblemHolding guard only blocks; two foot soldiers killed the Wolf in 20–30 s. About 25 attempts in one afternoon, no win.

    FixReading up showed that a deflect needs a fresh press of guard. Ignore the soldiers, run straight at the general, keep moving in the fight.

  5. 五

    Temple and Prosthetic

    寺院与忍义手Nov 11

    ReachedNov 11 · Through the scripted loss to Genichiro to the Dilapidated Temple: the Shinobi Prosthetic.

    ProblemReplaying recorded keys is open loop: one pass in three. After a fight the camera pointed down and the blind replay was 622 px off; two falls. The steps printed “OK” with nothing behind it.

    FixBefore a fight, save a frame of the way forward; afterwards turn until it matches. Every “OK” needs positive evidence on screen.

  6. 六

    General Kawarada

    忍殺

    河原田直盛Nov 11 — Nov 12

    ReachedNov 12, 17:16 · General Naomori Kawarada killed.

    ProblemLanding on the gatehouse roof, the Wolf was seen 3 times in 4; the red 危 warning was mistaken for the deathblow dot; head-on, four fights in five were lost.

    FixLearn the grapple marker: struck through means unusable, solid means go. Crouch 20 s, then drop for a plunging deathblow; head-on, hold guard and cut every 0.3 s.

    Chained Ogre

    忍殺

    赤鬼Nov 13 — Nov 21

Six things learned on the way

Quoted lines are the project lead's, translated from Chinese

  • Analyse before retrying

    “Stop retrying at random. Analyse more.”

    Find the cause frame by frame; change one thing at a time.

  • Use the cheap method

    “Too expensive. Use traditional methods.”

    Classical image processing and scripts throughout; not one neural network.

  • Close the loop

    “Open loop always fails. It basically never gets through.”

    Landmark → action → check; events you can see instead of timers.

  • Keep your own anchors

    “Before a fight, mark your direction: save a frame.”

    Frames the script saved itself match far better than someone else's video.

  • See the action happen

    Read the gourd count. Read the emblem count.

    Ten minutes of key tests at an idol solved what three days of statistics had not.

  • Attack more

    “Attack more. Stop thinking only of defence; attacking is what kills the enemy.”

    The lower his health, the faster his posture fills.
